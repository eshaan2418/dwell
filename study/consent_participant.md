## What is this study about?

We are testing *Dwell*, a research tool that estimates how quiet, how sunny and how hot an apartment
is from a short phone video plus public map data. We want to find out whether a Dwell report helps
people choose apartments that match what they say they want, and how people use the uncertainty
ranges in the report.

## What will I be asked to do?

One session of about **30 minutes**, in person or by video call:

1. Tell us how much you care about quiet, winter sun and staying cool in summer (about 2 minutes).
2. Look at about 6 to 8 pairs of real apartments. For each pair you watch two short video tours, and
   for some pairs you also see a Dwell report. Then you choose the apartment you would rather live in,
   say how confident you are, and sometimes estimate a probability (for example "how likely is it that
   apartment A is quieter?").
3. Answer a few short questions about the reports.

There are no right or wrong answers. We are testing the tool, not you.

## About the apartments you will see

The apartments belong to volunteers who gave written consent for their unit to be shown, anonymously,
in this study. You will not be told addresses, and the videos have been checked and blurred so that
people, documents and personal photos cannot be seen. **Please do not try to identify the apartments
or their occupants, and do not record, photograph or share anything shown to you in the session.** If
you think you recognise an apartment, or you know someone who lives in one, please tell the
researcher. This is not a problem, but we will leave your answers out of the analysis.

The apartments are **not for rent** through this study. The reports are research estimates and come
with uncertainty. They are not building inspections.

## What data will be collected and how is it stored?

- Your answers: preference points and ranking, choices, confidence ratings, probability estimates,
  questionnaire answers and any comments you choose to make. Optionally your age band and whether you
  currently rent, if the board requires demographics.
- **We do not record audio or video of you.** If the session runs over a video call it is not recorded.
- Your answers are stored under a **code** (for example `P07`), never under your name. The only link
  between your name and your code is the signed consent form, which is kept separately, on paper in
  a locked place or as an encrypted file. It is never stored with the study data and never in any
  shared or public repository.
- The coded data are kept on the researcher's encrypted computer for `<retention period, e.g. 5
  years>` and then deleted. Published results report only group-level statistics. A de-identified
  dataset (codes, answers, no names, no free-text comments that could identify you) may be shared
  with other researchers or published alongside a paper **only if you tick the box below**.

Risks are minimal: the session may be slightly tiring. You can take a break at any time. There is no
direct benefit to you, but your participation helps evaluate whether tools like this help renters.
